About Us

A Warm Welcome
Awaits You

Bangkok Travel Suites Hotel adds warmth to urban living with the care and Thai hospitality of our staff. Our 40 spacious rooms are decorated in a contemporary style, offering the most flexible and comfortable accommodation in Nonthaburi.

Located just 20 minutes from Don Mueang International Airport and conveniently situated on Ngamwongwan Road near the expressway, we provide easy access to hospitals, tourist attractions, and government centers.
